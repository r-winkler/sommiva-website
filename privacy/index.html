Privacy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

In short

Sommiva needs no account, no e-mail address and no phone number. The app contains no advertising, no tracking and no analytics tools. It stores only what is needed to count together: display name, amounts, notes, areas and an anonymous user ID. The data is stored in Zurich and is deleted automatically after a fixed period.

Responsible

René Winkler
E-mail: contact@sommiva.com

What data

When you create or join a collection, Sommiva stores in the cloud:

  • Display name: the name you enter. A nickname is enough.
  • Amounts and notes: what you record, with time and area. Please do not write names or addresses of other people in notes.
  • Collection and areas: name, currency, goal, end date and visibility of the collection, its areas (e.g. "Bar" or "Group North") and who is a lead or a member.
  • Change log: corrections and deletions of entries, with time and who made them.
  • Anonymous user ID: a random identifier so that your entries are assigned to you. It contains no name and no contact details.
  • Comparison with a template: when the lead creates a collection from an earlier collection as a template, the new collection keeps that collection's sums per day and per area and its total, without names, notes, user IDs or single entries. These sums stay with the new collection until it is deleted, even if the earlier collection is deleted before. Only the lead sees them.
  • Moving to a new device: when you move Sommiva to a new device, a short-lived transfer record is created with your anonymous user ID, a hash of the code (not the code itself), times and, once scanned, the anonymous ID of the new device. It is deleted at the latest one day after the code expired (10 minutes). The new device's previous anonymous identifier is deleted in the process. Settings are not transferred.

Your settings (e.g. language, appearance, number format) and your suggested display name stay on your device only.

Purpose and legal basis

We process this data only so that you can record amounts together and see the total. The legal basis is providing the app you want to use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; in Switzerland the Federal Act on Data Protection). We do not analyse, sell or use the data for advertising.

Where the data is stored

Sommiva uses Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, anonymous sign-in and Cloud Functions for automatic deletion and moving to a new device) by Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC. The data of collections is stored in the Zurich region (europe-west6), and the Cloud Functions run there. For anonymous sign-in, Google may process technical data such as the anonymous user ID and the IP address outside Switzerland and the EU as well, in particular in the USA; Google relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses of the European Commission for this. Google processes the data as a processor under the Firebase data processing terms.

Camera and QR codes

Sommiva uses the camera only to scan QR codes for joining a collection or moving to a new device. The camera image is processed on your device and is neither stored nor transmitted. On Android, the app recognises the codes with Google ML Kit. ML Kit sends information about the use and performance of the recognition to Google, such as device and app details; Google uses it to operate and improve ML Kit. On the iPhone, recognition uses Apple features on the device.

Who sees your data

Only the members of the same collection. The lead decides what members see: everything, only the total, or only their own amounts. The lead always sees everything. We do not pass any data on to third parties.

How long

A collection closes automatically after 30 days without a new entry (at the earliest 30 days after the end date), or when the lead closes it. Closed and automatically closed collections are deleted with all entries 24 months later; the app shows the deletion date on the collection. The lead can delete a collection at any time before that.

Recurring collections (weekly, monthly or in rounds closed by hand) close automatically after two rounds without an entry for the weekly and monthly rhythm, and after 30 days without an entry for rounds by hand. As long as a recurring collection exists, its rounds are deleted with all entries, sums and log records 24 months after the round ended; the collection itself continues.

Your rights

You can request access to your data, have it corrected or deleted, and object to its processing. In the app, you can delete directly under Settings › "Delete my data": your name becomes "Deleted member" in all collections and your notes are deleted. Your amounts remain so that the collection's total stays correct.

If the lead removes you from a collection, your entries remain visible with your name and the note "removed", so that the accounts stay traceable.

For anything else, write to contact@sommiva.com. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in Switzerland with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

Purchases

You buy Sommiva Pro through Google Play or the App Store. The store handles the payment; we receive no payment data. The app only asks the store whether Pro was bought.

This website

The website sommiva.com and the page for invitation links sommiva.app set no cookies, use no tracking and load nothing from other servers. They are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When a page is requested, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as the IP address to deliver the page and protect it against attacks. When an invitation link is opened in a browser, this includes the invitation code in the address; it only allows joining one collection and expires after 7 days. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Changes

If the app changes so that other data is processed, we update this policy and the date above.
