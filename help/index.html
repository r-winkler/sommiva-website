Help

Answers to the most common questions about Sommiva. Your question is not here? Write to us.

Getting started

How do I start a collection?

On the start screen, tap “New collection”. Enter a name and the currency, and optionally a goal and an end date. Whoever creates the collection is its lead. Inside the collection, Sommiva shows short tips at first; Settings › “Show tips again” brings them back.

How do I invite others?

Open the collection and tap “Invite”. The others scan the QR code with their camera or with Sommiva, or you share the link in a chat.

Every invitation has an expiry date. “Revoke invitation” makes the code and the link invalid right away.

Does everyone need an account?

No. Whoever joins only enters a display name. There is no sign-up and no password, and no e-mail is needed. If you like, you can link an e-mail to get your collections back should your device ever be missing (see Get collections back with an e-mail).

Entering amounts

How do I enter an amount?

In the collection, tap “Record”, type the amount and tap “Record” again. A note and an area are optional. The shared total goes up right away.

I made a typo. What now?

You can correct or delete your own entries. The lead can correct all entries. Every change is kept in the log, so the total stays traceable.

What happens without internet?

You record as usual. The entries are on your phone right away and are sent as soon as there is a connection again. The app shows what is still waiting.

What are areas?

Areas are stands or groups, for example “Bar” and “Grill” or “Group North”. The lead adds them under “Edit collection”. You then see the total per stand and overall. The free version allows two areas.

For the lead

Who sees how much I collected?

The lead decides: everyone sees everything, everyone sees only the total, or each person sees only their own amounts. The lead always sees everything.

What if my phone gets lost or I delete the app?

Appoint a second lead early: under “Members”, choose a person and make them a lead. All leads have the same rights, so the collection stays in good hands.

Without the old device, your collections only come back if you linked an e-mail beforehand: tap “Recover with e-mail” on the empty start screen (see Get collections back with an e-mail). Otherwise only a second lead can keep the collection going, because Sommiva has no account.

The same applies if you delete the app, clear its storage or reset your phone: Sommiva no longer recognizes you afterwards. So link an e-mail early (Settings › “Link e-mail”), move your collections to another device first (Settings › “Move to a new device”) or appoint a second lead.

When does a collection end?

When the lead closes it, or automatically after 30 days without a new entry (at the earliest 30 days after the end date). The end date is only a reminder: latecomers can still record afterwards.

Seven days before it closes automatically, the app shows all members a notice with the date. The lead can reopen a collection that closed automatically.

Data and security

I have a new phone. How do I take my collections along?

You need both devices. On the old device, tap “Move to a new device” in Settings; a QR code appears that is valid for 10 minutes. On the new device, tap “Take over from old device” in Settings and scan the code. Afterwards the new device has all collections with your roles and amounts, and the old device starts empty.

Settings such as language and colour theme stay on each device. Get Pro back on the new device with “Restore purchase” (see below).

How do I get my collections back with an e-mail?

Beforehand, link your e-mail address under Settings › “Link e-mail” and confirm it with the 6-digit code from the mail. Sommiva stores only a fingerprint of the address, not the address itself, and only writes to you when you request a code.

If your device is missing or you reinstalled the app, tap “Recover with e-mail” on the empty start screen (or in Settings), enter the same address and then the code from the mail. Afterwards all collections are back with your roles and amounts; another device with these collections is signed out. Confirmed Pro comes along, otherwise get it back with “Restore purchase”. Only link an address that only you can access.

How long is the data kept?

A collection is deleted with all its entries 24 months after it was closed or closed automatically. The app shows the deletion date on the closed collection. The lead can delete a collection earlier at any time. In recurring collections, each round is deleted 24 months after it ended, while the collection continues.

How do I delete my data?

In the app under Settings › “Delete my data”. Your name becomes “Deleted member”, your notes are deleted and a linked e-mail is removed. Your amounts stay, so the collection’s total remains correct. More in the privacy policy.

Is Sommiva a cash register?

No. Sommiva is a counting and collecting tool for clubs and groups. It processes no payments, prints no receipts and does not replace a cash register.

Free and Pro

What does the free version include?

1 active collection as lead with up to 5 members and 2 areas, without ads and without a time limit. As a member, you can join as many collections as you like.

What does Pro offer, and what does it cost?

Pro is a one-time purchase without a subscription. You see the price for your country in the app and in the store. Only those who lead collections need Pro. Among other things, Pro unlocks unlimited collections, members and areas, statistics, and export as CSV and PDF.

I have a new phone. How do I get Pro back?

Sign in to the App Store or Google Play with the same account you used for the purchase. Then, in the app, go to Settings › “Sommiva Pro” and tap “Restore purchase”.

What happens to Pro if Sommiva is discontinued?

We do not plan to. Because Sommiva needs a server for the shared total, we still promise clearly how it would go: we announce it at least 6 months in advance in the app and on this website, stop selling Pro from then on and unlock export as CSV and PDF for everyone, so that you can save your figures. After that, we delete all data.

Question not answered?

Write to us. If something does not work, the app version helps us: you find it in the app under Settings › “About Sommiva”.
