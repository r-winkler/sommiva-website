What Sommiva does

  • Live total

    Overall, per person and per area – on every phone at the same time.

  • Works without a connection

    In the cellar, out in the field, in a dead zone: recording always works. It syncs as soon as there is a connection.

  • Traceable

    Corrections and deletions stay visible in the change log. The accounts add up – and everyone sees why.

  • You decide who sees what

    Everything, only the total or only one’s own amounts. No competition unless you want it.

  • Several leads

    Appoint a second lead. If a phone gets lost, the collection can carry on.

  • Close it cleanly

    Once everything is counted, the lead closes the collection. After that it is read-only.

Frequently asked questions

Does everyone need an account?

No. Whoever joins only enters a display name. There is no sign-up, no e-mail and no password.

What happens without internet?

You record as usual. The entries are on your phone right away and are sent as soon as there is a connection again. The app shows what is still waiting.

Is Sommiva a cash register?

No. Sommiva is a counting and collecting tool for clubs and groups. It processes no payments, prints no receipts and does not replace a cash register.

Who sees how much I collected?

The lead decides: everyone sees everything, everyone sees only the total, or each person sees only their own amounts. The lead always sees everything.

How long is the data kept?

A collection runs as long as you record. After 30 days without an entry it closes automatically. Closed collections are deleted after 24 months – the app reminds you beforehand. In recurring collections, rounds are deleted 24 months after they ended.

What does Pro cost?

Pro is a one-time purchase without a subscription. You see the price for your country in the app and in the store. Only those who lead collections need Pro.
