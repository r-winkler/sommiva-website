Terms of use

Last updated: 9 October 2026

In short

Sommiva is a tool for a group to count cash income together. Use it only for purposes that are allowed. If someone uses Sommiva for something unlawful, we can block their invitations and collections. This is a translation; the German version prevails in case of differences.

What Sommiva is

Sommiva records amounts your group takes in and shows the common total. The app moves no money, processes no payments, issues no receipts and replaces neither a cash register nor bookkeeping. You are responsible for the cash itself, its accounting and any taxes or permits.

The provider is René Winkler, Switzerland, reachable at contact@sommiva.com.

What is not allowed

You must not use Sommiva for unlawful purposes, in particular not:

  • to hide income from tax or other authorities,
  • for illegal gambling,
  • for fake or misleading fundraising,
  • for extortion or protection money,
  • to exploit people, for example through forced begging or work.

Do not use other people's names or misleading names for collections, and do not write details about other people in notes that do not belong there.

Who is responsible for what

The lead of a collection is responsible for its purpose being allowed and for inviting only people who are meant to take part. Every member is responsible for their own entries and notes.

Reports and blocking

If you notice a collection that breaks these terms, write to contact@sommiva.com, ideally with the invitation link. If there are concrete indications of a breach, we can revoke invitations, close the collection and refuse further use without prior notice.

We do not check notes or entries on our own initiative. We disclose data to authorities only on a valid order under Swiss law, and only what is stored (see the privacy policy).

Availability and data

We do our best to keep Sommiva working, but we guarantee neither constant availability nor that no data is lost. Collections are deleted automatically after the periods stated in the privacy policy. Keep important results yourself, for example with the export.

Liability

As far as the law allows, we are not liable for damage arising from the use of Sommiva, such as wrong totals, missing cash or lost data. We are liable for intent and gross negligence as provided by law.

Law
